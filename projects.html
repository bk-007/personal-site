Selected work / Generalized for confidentiality

Work that changes the work.

These examples show how I use leadership, process improvement, quality systems, data, and practical technology to close the gap between the intended process and the real one.

Manufacturing data, employer details, and internal process specifics are intentionally omitted. The operating problems and methods are real.

Building a capable, accountable quality team

I built a stronger team culture through clear expectations, regular one-on-ones, meaningful recognition, cross-training, and delegated ownership. The goal was not simply to get more work done. It was to help people make sound decisions, take pride in their work, and grow into greater responsibility.

I have led improvements across measurement methods, machine ownership, incoming inspection, defect containment, testing, materials, and cross-functional handoffs.
